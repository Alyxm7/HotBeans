Front-End Developer

We are seeking a skilled and enthusiastic Front-End Developer who is passionate about creating beautiful, functional, and responsive web interfaces. As part of our team, you will work closely with designers, back-end developers, and project managers to build modern, high-performance websites and web applications that deliver exceptional user experiences.


Requirements:


  • Proficient in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • Strong understanding of responsive design and mobile-first development
  • Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment
  • Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail


Qualifications:


  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
  • Portfolio of front-end development work
  • 1+ years of professional experience in web development
  • Familiarity with frameworks such as React or Vue.js is a plus

Back-End Developer

We're looking for a talented Back-End Developer to join our team and play a key role in building the server-side architecture for the web applications we create. In this role, you will design and develop the core components, databases, and APIs that power our client projects. Collaborating with front-end developers, designers, and project managers, you'll help deliver seamless, high-performance web solutions that are reliable, secure, and scalable.


Requirements:


  • Proficient in server-side languages (e.g., Node.js, Python, PHP)
  • Experience with database technologies (e.g., MySQL, MongoDB)
  • Strong understanding of RESTful APIs and web services
  • Ability to optimize application performance and scalability


Qualifications:


  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field
  • 2+ years of back-end development experience
  • Experience working with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure) is a plus
  • Strong understanding of software architecture and design patterns
